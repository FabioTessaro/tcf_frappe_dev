# 703 · Coverage roadmap

## Purpose

Which app covers which norm, in which order. Updated whenever an app is planned or released.

## Phases (initial plan, adjustable)

| Phase | Deliverable | Norms covered (fully or partly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 · Platform | `<core-app>` with security model, Party, capabilities, files, audit, e-signatures, records, privacy, incidents; delivery pipeline | NIS2, CRA, ISO 27001/27002, ISO 27701, ISO 15489/16175 (platform level) |
| 1 · Management-system foundation | `<ims-app>` (pending decision): documents, risks, objectives, competence, internal audits, management review, NCR/CAPA | Common clauses of EN 9100, ISO 14001/45001/50001/27001; EN 9101 (audits); EN 9136 (root cause) |
| 2 · Quality | QMS app: FAI, APQP/PPAP, supplier quality | EN 9100, EN 9102, EN 9145 |
| 3 · People | HR and training/qualification app | EN 4179, EN 9100 §7.2 |
| 4 · Engineering and production | PLM, MES | EN 9100 §8.1.2, ISO 10007, EN 9103, EN 9146, Nadcap |
| 5 · Maintenance | MRO app | EN 9110 |
| 6 · EHS and energy | EMS, OHS, EnMS apps | ISO 14001, 45001, 50001 specifics |
| 7 · Measurement | Calibration / lab app | ISO 10012, ISO/IEC 17025 |

## Rules

- `CMP-RM-001` A phase is complete only when its traceability rows are `implemented` and evidence
  exists for each.
